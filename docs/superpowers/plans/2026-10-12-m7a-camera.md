# M7a Camera Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The camera can be orbited, panned, zoomed, fitted to the model, set to standard views and switched to parallel (orthographic) projection, by agents (commands) and in the window (Camera menu, Shift+Z), with every tool and the renderer working in parallel projection.

**Architecture:**
- kernel: unchanged.
- session: `Camera` gains `projection` and the parallel math plus helpers (`depth`,
  `depth_range`, `sees_depth`, `pixel_size`, `eye_distance`, `sight_origin`, `ground_at`) that
  every consumer uses instead of perspective formulas; `turn` (angles), `zoom_by`, `look_from`,
  `fit`. Camera commands live in `document/camera.rs` (`moved_camera`, `camera_for`); not undo
  steps; `.skr` schema 4.
- tools: inference occlusion from `sight_origin`, index cone with `r0` for parallel rays,
  ground via `ground_at`; overlay/select/rotate sizes via `pixel_size`/`eye_distance`.
- render: uniforms carry a headlight point or direction and a depth-offset mode; guide crosses
  via camera helpers.
- api: `render_view` camera takes `up` and `projection`; describe schemas pick up the new
  commands automatically.
- app: Camera menu, Shift+Z, Erase Selection in the Edit menu; editor camera commands keep the
  tool running.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 2024; no dependency changes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-tenon-design.md`:
- the camera and navigation sections;
- section 5 (commands and queries, `render_view`).

## Spec notes (rulings made in this plan)

1. **Camera commands are not undo steps** (spec section 5 already says so). They return empty
   changes, do not bump the revision, send no `changed` notification, and `Command::changes_model`
   is false for them (so the editor does not reset the tool). In an atomic batch they run through
   the existing `None => self.execute(command)` arm and a failure restores the camera (existing
   `camera_before` rollback). New `Command::is_camera()` lists them.
2. **New commands** (all in `session::Command`, snake_case JSON):
   - `orbit {yaw?, pitch?}`: degrees, both default 0. Yaw about +Z through the target,
     counter-clockwise seen from above; pitch raises the eye (internally `turn(target, yaw,
     -pitch)`). Pitch clamps `MIN_POLAR` (0.01 rad) short of straight down/up, as the mouse
     orbit already did. About the target only (no pivot argument; the mouse keeps its
     point-under-cursor pivot).
   - `pan {right?, up?}`: meters along the camera's screen right/up; moves eye and target.
   - `zoom {factor}`: eye to `distance / factor` along the view (factor > 1 zooms in), never
     nearer than `MIN_ZOOM_DISTANCE` (0.01 m). Non-positive or non-finite factor: invalid_input.
   - `zoom_extents`: whole model, never the selection (SketchUp's Zoom Extents; Zoom Selection
     deferred). Bounds = `Scene::bounds(MeshId::Root)` (root vertices + placed instance boxes,
     root coordinates, whatever instance is open). Guides do not count (bounds leave them out;
     guide lines are infinite). Empty model (or guides only): Ok, camera unchanged (no error,
     like SketchUp). Keeps the view direction. Perspective fits the bounding sphere
     (`d = r / sin(min(half_fov_y, half_fov_x))`); parallel fits the box's 8 corners projected
     on right/up with `FIT_MARGIN` 1.05. Distance at least `MIN_ZOOM_DISTANCE`.
   - `standard_view {view}`: top, bottom, front, back, left, right, iso. Keeps target and distance
     and projection (SketchUp does not zoom extents on a view change). Directions (target to
     eye, screen up): top (+Z, +Y), bottom (-Z, +Y), front (-Y, +Z), back (+Y, +Z), left (-X, +Z),
     right (+X, +Z), iso ((-1,-1,1)/sqrt3, +Z) = the default camera's quadrant.
   - `set_projection {projection}`: `perspective` | `parallel`.
   - Every camera command checks the resulting eye and target are within `MAX_COORDINATE`;
     otherwise invalid_input "the camera would move beyond the coordinate limit" and the camera
     stays (also covers zoom_extents on a model near the limit).
3. **`set_camera` gains `up?` and `projection?`**; `fov_y_deg` and `projection` keep the current
   values when absent. Vertical views are now **allowed** (before: invalid_input "view direction
   must not be vertical"). Vertical = `|forward x Z| < VERTICAL_TOLERANCE (1e-9)`. A vertical view
   takes its screen up from the horizontal part of `up` (default +Y); a vertical or zero `up`
   is invalid_input. Any other view has `up = Z` and ignores `up` (documented). `eye == target`
   stays invalid_input. Existing test `commands.rs::set_camera_moves_camera_without_history`
   changed accordingly; `api/tests/dispatch.rs` render_view failure case switched to eye ==
   target.
4. **Camera invariant**: `Camera.up` is `UP` except in a vertical view, where it is the horizontal
   screen-up (and except cameras made by `placed`). `Camera::turn` (orbit) spins `up` with the
   yaw while the view stays vertical and resets it to `UP` once it tilts; from a vertical view the
   pitch can only tilt toward the top of the screen (clamp range starts at 0), and `right()` does
   not flip when leaving the vertical (tested).
5. **Parallel projection model**: no new stored size. The view height is derived:
   `view_height = 2 * distance * tan(fov_y / 2)` (the perspective view's height at the target),
   so toggling projection keeps the target's apparent size, zoom in parallel = moving the eye
   (wheel zoom, `zoom`, `zoom_extents` all just set the distance), and `fov_y` is kept for
   switching back. The camera query reports `view_height` in both projections.
6. **Parallel depth range**: a parallel view sees `PARALLEL_DEPTH` (100) eye-to-target distances
   in front of and behind the target, **including geometry behind the eye** (the eye only sets
   the zoom). `depth_range()` = (d - 100d, d + 100d); perspective = (near, inf). Projection is
   glam `directx::orthographic(-w, w, -h, h, far, near)` (near/far swapped = reversed depth,
   1 at the near end, 0 at the far end, linear). 100 (not `LINE_REACH` 1000) keeps f32 depth
   precision: edge/face resolution about 1.2e-5 d.
7. **Rays in parallel** start on the near end of the depth range (`eye + forward * near`, offset
   on right/up) and run along `forward`, so picking/inference/face hits see geometry behind the
   eye exactly as rendered. `project` returns None outside the depth range in parallel
   (perspective unchanged: None behind the eye).
8. **Perspective-only assumptions replaced by camera helpers** (perspective results unchanged):
   `depth`, `depth_range`, `sees_depth` (replaces `depth <= near()` checks), `pixel_size(p)`
   (replaces `2 * depth * tan(fov/2) / viewport.y`: snap marker, guide crosses in render and in
   Select/Erase/Move outlines, pan), `eye_distance(p)` (protractor; parallel = distance()),
   `sight_origin(p)` (inference occlusion `visible`), `ground_at(px)` (ground snap within
   `MAX_ANCHOR_FACTOR`; in parallel measured in depth from the target, else every ground hit was
   rejected because ray origins are 100 d back), `anchor_at` fallback (target depth),
   `line_span` (parallel: within the depth range minus a 0.1% margin, along-line reach around
   the foot from the target), `zoom` (parallel clamps on distance()). Index cone for snapping
   (`Near::around`): parallel rays do not spread, so the cone gets `r0 = 2 * |beside.origin -
   ray.origin|` (0 in perspective) besides the slope.
9. **Rendering**: `Uniforms` gains `depth_offset: [f32; 4]`; the headlight `eye` uniform is
   `(eye, 1)` in perspective and `(-forward, 0)` in parallel; the shader uses
   `normalize(u.eye.xyz - in.world * u.eye.w)`. Line depth offsets: perspective keeps
   `clip.z * depth_scale` (x = 1); parallel adds `(depth_scale - 1) * d / (far - near)` (y),
   i.e. the same 0.05% of the eye-to-target distance at any depth, instead of scaling linear
   depth (which moved edges by 0.5 d and showed them through faces).
10. **`.skr` schema version 4**: `camera` gets `up` (written always, `Option` on read) and
    `projection` (`#[serde(default)]` perspective). Bumped because `StoredCamera` has
    `deny_unknown_fields`: an older app must say "newer schema version", not "unknown field".
    Versions 1-3 open in perspective with Z up. A file whose view is unusable still opens with
    the default view. `curves.rs` test that rewrites a file to version 2 now also strips the
    camera's new fields.
11. **`render_view`'s camera** takes `up` and `projection` too (same `Document::camera_for`
    checks, absent fields keep the document camera's values); never moves the document camera.
12. **UI**: a Camera menu after Edit: Zoom Extents (shortcut text Shift+Z), separator, Top,
    Bottom, Front, Back, Left, Right, Iso, separator, Perspective / Parallel Projection as
    selectable labels checked by the current projection (`View.projection`). No toolbar buttons
    and no single-key shortcuts for views (SketchUp has none by default). Shift+Z arrives as
    `EditorKey::Char('Z')` (layout applies Shift; non-ASCII layouts via the new
    `KeyCode::KeyZ => 'z'` physical fallback, uppercased with Shift). Camera moves from the
    menu or Shift+Z keep the tool's operation and typed text (`run_camera_command`), and clear
    the status message. `Editor::set_projection` returns false when already in that
    projection.
13. **Toolbar width**: the Camera menu would push the toolbar to about 1300 pt (Close group
    clipped at the 1280 x 800 test window, measured by scanning clicks on a 1600 pt screen). So
    **Erase Selection moves from the toolbar to the Edit menu** (first item, shortcut text
    Delete, enabled with a selection; SketchUp has Edit > Delete). Toolbar now about 1195 pt.
    The Edit menu tests were rewritten around a shared `menu_actions` helper.

```rust
//! Camera commands (orbit, pan, zoom, zoom extents, standard views, projection) and the camera
//! query, driven through JSON commands the way the API sends them.

use std::io::Write;
use std::path::PathBuf;

use glam::{DVec2, DVec3};
use serde_json::{Value, json};
use session::{Command, Document, ErrorKind, Query};

fn doc() -> Document {
    Document::new(DVec2::new(800.0, 600.0))
}

fn run(d: &mut Document, v: Value) -> Result<Value, ErrorKind> {
    let cmd: Command = serde_json::from_value(v).expect("valid command json");
    d.execute(cmd)
        .map(|o| serde_json::to_value(o).unwrap())
        .map_err(|e| e.kind)
}

fn ask(d: &Document, q: Value) -> Value {
    let q: Query = serde_json::from_value(q).unwrap();
    serde_json::to_value(d.query(&q).unwrap()).unwrap()
}

fn vec3(v: &Value) -> DVec3 {
    let a: [f64; 3] = serde_json::from_value(v.clone()).unwrap();
    DVec3::from(a)
}

/// The camera query's eye, target and up.
fn view(d: &Document) -> (DVec3, DVec3, DVec3) {
    let c = ask(d, json!({"type": "camera"}));
    (vec3(&c["eye"]), vec3(&c["target"]), vec3(&c["up"]))
}

fn close(a: DVec3, b: DVec3) -> bool {
    a.abs_diff_eq(b, 1e-9)
}

/// Looking along +Y at the origin from 10 m away.
fn front(d: &mut Document) {
    run(
        d,
        json!({"type": "set_camera", "eye": [0, -10, 0], "target": [0, 0, 0]}),
    )
    .unwrap();
}

/// A 2 x 3 x 1.5 m box with its corner at the origin.
fn block(d: &mut Document) {
    run(
        d,
        json!({"type": "add_rectangle", "corner": [0, 0, 0], "opposite": [2, 3, 0]}),
    )
    .unwrap();
    let face = ask(d, json!({"type": "entities", "kinds": ["face"]}))["faces"][0]["id"].clone();
    run(
        d,
        json!({"type": "push_pull", "face": face, "distance": 1.5}),
    )
    .unwrap();
}

#[test]
fn orbit_turns_the_eye_about_the_target() {
    let mut d = doc();
    front(&mut d);
    let out = run(&mut d, json!({"type": "orbit", "yaw": 90})).unwrap();
    assert_eq!(out["changes"]["created"], json!([]));
    let (eye, target, up) = view(&d);
    assert!(close(eye, DVec3::new(10.0, 0.0, 0.0)), "{eye}");
    assert!(close(target, DVec3::ZERO) && close(up, DVec3::Z));
    run(&mut d, json!({"type": "orbit", "pitch": 30})).unwrap();
    let (eye, _, _) = view(&d);
    let (s, c) = 30f64.to_radians().sin_cos();
    assert!(close(eye, DVec3::new(10.0 * c, 0.0, 10.0 * s)), "{eye}");
    assert_eq!(ask(&d, json!({"type": "history"}))["undo_depth"], 0);
    assert_eq!(d.revision(), 0);
}

#[test]
fn orbit_stops_short_of_straight_down() {
    let mut d = doc();
    front(&mut d);
    run(&mut d, json!({"type": "orbit", "pitch": 200})).unwrap();
    let (eye, target, up) = view(&d);
    let polar = (target - eye).angle_between(-DVec3::Z);
    assert!((polar - 0.01).abs() < 1e-9, "{polar}");

#[test]
fn pan_moves_eye_and_target_along_the_screen() {

#[test]
fn standard_views_look_along_the_axes_from_the_same_distance() {
    let mut d = doc();
    front(&mut d);
    run(&mut d, json!({"type": "pan", "right": 1, "up": 2})).unwrap();
    let t = DVec3::new(1.0, 0.0, 2.0);
    let cases = [
        ("top", DVec3::Z, DVec3::Y),
        ("bottom", -DVec3::Z, DVec3::Y),
        ("front", -DVec3::Y, DVec3::Z),
        ("back", DVec3::Y, DVec3::Z),
        ("left", -DVec3::X, DVec3::Z),
        ("right", DVec3::X, DVec3::Z),
        ("iso", DVec3::new(-1.0, -1.0, 1.0).normalize(), DVec3::Z),
    ];
    for (name, from, screen_up) in cases {
        run(&mut d, json!({"type": "standard_view", "view": name})).unwrap();
        let (eye, target, up) = view(&d);
        assert!(close(target, t), "{name}: {target}");
        assert!(close(eye, t + from * 10.0), "{name}: {eye}");
        assert!(close(up, screen_up), "{name}: {up}");
    }
    assert!(
        serde_json::from_value::<Command>(json!({"type": "standard_view", "view": "side"}))
            .is_err()
    );
}

#[test]
fn a_top_view_orbits_by_spinning_then_tilting() {
    let mut d = doc();
    run(&mut d, json!({"type": "standard_view", "view": "top"})).unwrap();
    run(&mut d, json!({"type": "orbit", "yaw": 90})).unwrap();

    assert!(close(
        eye - target,
        DVec3::new(0.0, 0.0, eye.distance(target))
    ));
    assert!(
        close(up, -DVec3::X),

    let (eye, target, up) = view(&d);
    assert!(close(up, DVec3::Z), "a tilted view has Z up: {up}");
    // Tilted toward what was the top of the screen (-X): the eye moved to +X.
    assert!(eye.x > target.x + 1.0 && eye.y.abs() < 1e-9, "{eye}");
}

#[test]

    run(
        &mut d,

    )
    .unwrap();

        ask(&d, json!({"type": "camera"}))["projection"],

    );
}

#[test]
fn zoom_extents_fits_the_whole_model() {
```

## Self-review notes

- **Spec coverage:** camera navigation and projection (commands: Task 1; tools and render: Task 2; window: Task 3).
- **Type names used across tasks:** `Projection`, `StandardView`, `Camera::{is_parallel, view_height, pixel_size, eye_distance, sight_origin, sees_depth, depth_range, ground_at, turn, zoom_by, look_from, fit}`, `Command::{Orbit, Pan, Zoom, ZoomExtents, StandardView, SetProjection}`, `UiAction::{ZoomExtents, StandardView, SetProjection}`.
- **Deferred** (from the scratch build):

Deferred (M8 or later):
- Zoom Selection / zoom to given ids; zoom window; field-of-view tool; camera undo (SketchUp's
  Previous/Next view).
- Animated transitions between views (spec "camera animation").
- Zoom extents with an eye beyond 1e6 m is refused rather than clamped (huge models only).
- Parallel depth range fixed at 100 eye-to-target distances: zoomed far into a detail of a big
  model, parts more than 100 d away along the view are clipped. A depth range from the model's
  bounds would fix it (needs the scene in the camera or a stored range).
- Push/pull drag along a normal parallel to the view (top view, horizontal face) has no
  measurable movement (same as a perspective top-ish view; SketchUp asks you to orbit).
- `Camera.up` field vs `up()` method naming (already deferred) now matters more: `up` is the
  screen-up hint, `up()` the computed one.
- Mouse orbit from a vertical view can only tilt toward the top of the screen (a drag the other
  way is clamped), by design of the pitch clamp.
- Select tool's rubber band is drawn half a distance in front of the ray origins (in parallel,
  near the back of the depth range); fine because overlays ignore depth.
- No select-tool unit test for guide_outline in parallel (same helpers as render guides, which
  are tested).
- Camera changes are not streamed to `subscribe` (pre-existing: no camera notification).
- Toolbar is at about 1195 pt of 1280; the next toolbar addition needs another menu move or a
  second row.
