# M18e The Working V6 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the V6 example into a working mechanism: the crank drives rods and pistons, the cams turn at half speed, the valves follow the cam lobes, the whole engine runs a 720 degree cycle as rendered frames, and the script reports interference, clearances, displacement and mass.

**Architecture:** Python client helpers for the M18 commands and queries; a `render_view` option to hide the ground grid and axes (the gap the M17g example hit); the V6 example rebuilt on a rigged assembly: each moving part a root instance, joints, couplings, cam followers, densities on materials; a cycle script that renders frames with `render_view { joints }`, runs `sweep_check`, `clearance`, `mass_properties` and `measure`, and writes a short report.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.9 standard library; Rust 2024 for the one render option. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/roadmap.md` row M18e. Depends on M18a-M18d.

## Global Constraints

## Spec notes (rulings)

1. **`render_view { ground: false }`** (default true): hides the ground grid, the axes and ground shadows for that render (the style's ground colour as well when the sky is off), so a model can sit at z = 0. Window unchanged. Docs and MCP schema.
2. **Client helpers** (`clients/python/sketchup_alt/model.py`): `Model.joint(kind, a, b, point, axis, reference=None, name=None, limits=None) -> Joint`; `Joint.drive(value, slide=None)`, `.value`, `.delete()`; `Model.couple(driver, driven, ratio, offset=None)`; `Model.cam_follower(cam, follower_joint, roller_radius=0, preload_direction=None)`; `Model.interference(ids=None, touching=False)`, `clearance(a, b)`, `sweep_check(joint, from_, to, steps, ids=None)`, `mass_properties(ids=None)`, `measure(distance=..|angle=..|round=..)`; `Material(..., density=)`; `Model.render(..., joints=None, ground=True)`; `Model.play` is window-only and not wrapped. Tests against the headless app.
3. **The rigged V6** (`clients/python/examples/v6_engine.py`, keeping its look from M17g): ground = block, heads, covers and accessories (root instances without joints, or joined fixed to the ground); crankshaft revolute to the ground on the main bearing axis; each rod revolute to its crank pin and revolute to its piston's wrist pin; each piston prismatic to the ground along its bore axis; each camshaft revolute to the ground, coupled 0.5 to the crank (timing so cylinder 1 is at TDC of compression when the crank is 0); each valve a prismatic joint along its stem, a cam follower on its cam lobe, preload toward the cam; sprockets and chains: sprockets fixed to their shafts (part of the crank or cam instances); the chain links move with nothing (static) in this milestone. Masses: steel 7850, cast iron 7200, aluminium 2700, copper 8960 on the materials.
4. **The cycle** (`clients/python/examples/v6_cycle.py`, or a `--cycle` flag): renders N frames (default 72, 10 degrees) from 0 to 720 with `render_view { joints: { crank: angle } }` from the cutaway camera into `frames/v6_000.png...`; also writes a contact sheet `v6-cycle.png` (a grid of 12 frames composed in pure Python by reading and writing PNG with zlib, or by requesting `render_view` at a small size per tile and stitching rows; stdlib only) and, if `ffmpeg` is on PATH, an `v6-cycle.mp4` (optional; skipped silently otherwise).
5. **The report** (`v6-report.txt` and printed): displacement from bore and stroke measured from the model (`measure round` on a bore wall, piston travel from `sweep_check`'s or the joint's range: stroke = piston position at BDC minus TDC), total mass and centre of gravity of the engine, crank assembly mass, `sweep_check` over 720 degrees (every pair with interference: there must be none; smallest piston-to-valve and piston-to-head clearances with the crank angles where they occur), compression ratio if the combustion chamber volume can be measured (swept volume plus chamber volume over chamber volume, chamber volume from `mass_properties` volume of a chamber solid built for the measurement; else omitted with a note).
6. **Acceptance:** the cycle runs with no solver refusal; `sweep_check` reports no interference between moving parts and the static block (clearances positive); valve lift curves follow the cam lobes (the report lists max lift per valve and the crank angle of max lift, one per 720 degrees); runtime of the build plus cycle under 2 minutes against a release app on this machine.

## Review Focus

1. Joints and couplings set up correctly (timing, firing order for a 60 degree V6: 1-2-3-4-5-6 with 120 degree crank pins as in the M17g geometry), valves never lose contact, no interference.
2. Report numbers plausible and derived from the model, not hard-coded.
3. Client helpers mirror the API and are tested; stdlib only; the render option documented.
