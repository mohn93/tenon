## openskp (record layouts and a fallback search ported into `crates/skp`; the writer in `crates/skpwrite`)

Some record layouts and the SketchUp 2020 count search in `crates/skp/src/legacy.rs` are
ported from openskp (https://github.com/iamahsanmehmood/openskp, commit
`13ae161fa0108647a04ad5730776120bb6fe8c83`); see `crates/skp/VENDORED.md`. The SketchUp
2017 writer in `crates/skpwrite` is ported from openskp's Python writer at the same commit;
see `crates/skpwrite/PORTED.md`. `crates/skpwrite/src/blank_v17.skp`, embedded in the
writer, is openskp's `_scaffold/blank_v17.skp` unchanged: a blank document saved by the
SketchUp SDK, as openskp describes it. openskp is distributed under the MIT licence:

## Test fixtures (not part of the app)

`crates/skp/tests/fixtures/cupboard.skp` and `desk.skp` are SketchUp models by Corynth,
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), from
https://corynth.itch.io/free-assets-sketchup-3d-assets, unchanged. `crates/skp/tests/fixtures/legacy/`
holds the cupboard saved in an older SketchUp file format (2017 to 2020) by SketchUp 2024. They are
used only by tests; see `crates/skp/tests/fixtures/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

## Fonts (built into the app)

`crates/session/assets/fonts` holds Inter 4.1 Regular (https://github.com/rsms/inter), which draws
3D text and the app's text, and `crates/app/assets/fonts` holds Inter 4.1 SemiBold and JetBrains
Mono 2.304 (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono), all licensed under the SIL Open Font
License, Version 1.1; sources and checksums are in the `README.md` beside them. The licence texts
follow.
